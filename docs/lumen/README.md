## Rendering

The tall convex Lumen geometry and coherent IOR 1.48 ray paths are unchanged. The new core uses a finite Gaussian line integral along each refracted/internal-reflected segment: a narrow white spine, wider icy mantle and faint aura. This removes the old 12-sample core's potential to miss a narrow centerline and provides continuous optical depth. Exposure 1.12 and bloom strength .18 / radius .35 / threshold 1.6 remain unchanged; luminosity comes from integrated emission rather than an exposure increase.

A closed cylindrical proxy carries a 40-step front-to-back extinction/emission integral with analytic cylinder/cap intersection, soft radial falloff, a narrow focused center, broader atmosphere and slow low-frequency striation. The proxy and aperture follow the crown's actual world transform, including tilt and bob. A single bounded presentation light circuit supplies power, phase and energy to the spine, beam, orbital crests/collector nodes, helical particles, haze and derivative-filtered floor wavefronts. Local lights provide shared spill on physical surfaces. The upper ring is a collimating aperture; two lower rings orbit the crystal. The 1024² mip-filtered half-float live probe captures their changing light and beacon for refraction; refractors are hidden during capture to prevent feedback. Removed the unused legacy 512² probe from this Lumen-only scene.

Default dispersion remains off. Subtle and legacy settings are explicit comparisons; legacy may reproduce RGB artifacts. Navigation moves the camera through the existing three viewpoints. Inspection pulls the camera back and looks slightly higher to give the beacon space. Reduced motion freezes the shared clock and navigation poses while controls still trigger a static render. Optional renderer lifecycle and manual planner behavior are unchanged.

## Performance and limits

Raw [initial live](performance-live.txt), [frozen](performance-frozen.txt), [retry](performance-retry.txt), [final live](performance-final.txt) readings. Stage startup 188 ms to first submission, warm retry 102 ms. Steady submitted rate 165 FPS, p95 interval 6.3 ms; worst 58.6 ms initially, 17.0 ms in the later session. CPU submission samples 1.5–1.9 ms; live GPU samples 3.2–4.3 ms, with a 26.1 ms first-render/reinitialization sample. These are sampled timings, not GPU percentiles or display scanout, and warm local cache is not a cold-network test. First shader/program switches can cause stalls.

About 290–310 draw calls / 190k triangles including capture/postprocessing passes. 31 geometries/17 textures/31 programs in observed live states. Estimated HDR color targets 99.6 MiB, excluding depth, environment maps and driver/total VRAM. Renderer chunk 585.56 kB / 149.47 kB gzip. The six-face 1024² probe and per-pixel convex/volume tracing remain the principal recurring costs; reduced legacy allocation does not reduce requested optical features. No automatic fidelity downgrade.

The beacon uses fixed-step integration and conservative back-surface depth testing, not a scene-depth-aware participating-media renderer; foreground objects can conservatively clip volume contribution. Haze/caustics and surface light proxies are art-directed approximations, not global illumination or physical scattering. A single box-projected probe has near-field parallax/occlusion errors. Three internal segments omit residual bounces/polarization; fine ring silhouettes and critical angles can still shimmer (no TAA). The brightest spine/tip can clip at high power. HTML is composited separately, never refracted. No mobile/iPhone or offline-render fidelity claim. No staging/production deployment was made; existing Pages configuration targets the original prototype branch, not this experiment.

Implementation references: installed Three.js r186 ShaderMaterial, CylinderGeometry and render-target source; [ShaderMaterial](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/ShaderMaterial.html), [CubeCamera](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/CubeCamera.html). No academic logic, persistence, AI behavior, Canvas integration or V1 changes. Prior PRs remain open.
