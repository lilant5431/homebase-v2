## Art direction

These are original point layouts, not copied models or the attached Keystone topology. Both use closed convex hulls with planes derived from the actual rendered facets. The original V2.1C cut remains selectable. Comparison objects are arranged on the camera tangent so their left-to-right order survives orbit; the hero text is hidden during comparison to avoid covering a gem.

- **Lumen / precision lantern:** an octagonal, cut-corner section with alternating angular tiers, a narrowed shoulder, pointed crown and long pavilion. Large windows alternate with small bevel/kite facets, creating a tapered silhouette rather than the old parallel-sided prism. The refracted luminous spine is concentrated at its center.
- **Vesper / angular heart:** a deliberately broad five-sided equator, shallow tiered crown, flat pentagonal table and offset pointed pavilion. It is shorter, wider and less symmetrical than Lumen, with a compact glowing heart instead of a long spine. Its topology, proportions and plane hierarchy are substantively different.

Interpretive visual principles drawn from the requested references: [SEGA's Chaos Emerald imagery](https://asia.sega.com/SonicSuperStars/en/action/) suggests recognition through a small number of deliberate contour/facet decisions; [Arcane's documented magical color direction](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/features/arcane-season-two-behind-the-scenes) suggests a concentrated inner energy rather than indiscriminate glow; [Final Fantasy XVI's Mothercrystals](https://uk.finalfantasyxvi.com/?msi=false) suggest monumentality; [Land of the Lustrous's official Diamond character direction](https://www.land-of-the-lustrous.com/chara/diamond.html) suggests graphic clarity and expressive, selective highlights. These are design interpretations, not claims about those productions' rendering algorithms. The supplied Keystone art informed the calm axis and inner-light hierarchy, without being treated as an approved final mesh.

## Optical investigation and changes

V2.1C traced three different IORs independently. Near the critical angle, channels can take different total-internal-reflection branches, producing large RGB path disagreement rather than a fine edge spectrum. Its 512² unmipmapped capture, narrow high-contrast light stripes and 86-cycle growth striations also encouraged shimmer/aliasing. These are code-supported contributors, not an isolated GPU-driver diagnosis.

The default now traces **one achromatic RGB path at IOR 1.48**. Dispersion off is not fake transparency: entrance refraction, exact convex exit intersections, internal reflected continuations, Fresnel splitting and thickness absorption still run. Subtle ±0.00025 and legacy-strength ±0.005 modes permit explicit A/B testing; the latter intentionally reproduces objectionable splitting and is not recommended as the default.

New cuts use a **1024² × 6 half-float live capture with mipmaps and trilinear minification**. The original cut uses a 512² unmipmapped probe for comparison. Both probes are owned/disposed and framebuffer-validated; the original probe renders only when needed. All refractors are hidden during capture to avoid feedback. The new path offsets each continuation along its actual ray, preserves the sign of near-zero box-projection directions, and terminates missing intersections rather than inventing an exit. High-frequency face striation is removed from new materials. Broader studio illumination, restrained specular amplitude and bloom (.18 vs .4 strength) reduce glare without hiding missing refraction. Inner light is integrated along refracted paths, with distinct Lumen spine and Vesper diamond-shaped density. Floor caustics remain procedural artistic illumination, not a physical light transport solve.

**Comparison honesty:** “Original cut” means the V2.1C geometry under the experiment's shared lighting and stability corrections. It is not a pixel-identical replay of PR #4's render. Set legacy dispersion to inspect the former spectral separation; [the actual prior evidence](../crystal/README.md) remains preserved separately. Side-by-side mode uses the same environment probe origin with box-projected parallax: suitable for art-direction comparison, not exact near-field transport at three independent positions.

Cache state was warm; not a cold-network benchmark.

The increased capture resolution quadruples cube-face texels; mipmaps add another third. Gallery captures both probes, while normal new-cut viewing captures only the high-resolution probe. Three bounded internal segments still discard residual energy, use Schlick Fresnel, and omit birefringence/polarization/multiple scattering. Volume core and caustics are art-directed, not physical simulation. A single box-projected probe cannot exactly reproduce exit-position occlusion. Thin silhouettes and critical-angle transitions can still shimmer; no TAA or path tracing. HTML is composited separately, never refracted. No claim of matching the concept image's offline fidelity or of physical-device/mobile compatibility. Reduced motion retains static poses and reactive controls; no optimization removes requested optical features.

Reference implementation documentation: [Three.js CubeCamera](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/CubeCamera.html) and installed r186 camera/convex-geometry/render-target code. No merge, production deployment or replacement of the working planner interface.
